Kera Bus

Enhancing real-time of bus operations and tracking in Kerala.

Develop a mobile app that provides real-time tracking of buses, improves operational efficiency, and enhances the passenger experience in Kerala.

Context

The app is designed for users who need quick access to transportation-related information and services.

User Accounts: Users can create accounts, log in, and manage their profiles. Vehicle Information: Registration, fitness, tax, insurance, and emission details. Complaint System: A feature for registering and tracking complaints. Bus Search: A functionality for searching buses, including scheduling or fare details.

How could this impact users?

By creating a mobile app that allows users to track buses in real-time, the app provides commuters with certainty and saves valuable time.

User Interface

On analysing similar apps, I found that custom search options, quick bus schedule lookups, and clear route visualizers drastically improve user satisfaction.

  • Search by route, stop name, or bus operator.
  • Visual timeline of upcoming stops and live traffic delay notifications.
  • Instant ticket reservation right from schedule search results.

Pain Points

  • Trouble getting past steps in login / signup due to excess requirements.
  • Complaint registration might not be clear enough for first-time users.
  • Bus search function might not provide clear directions to reach a destination.

Improvements Suggested

  • Simplifying login / signup procedures.
  • Comprehensive complaint registration system with clear instructions and tracking options.
  • Improvements in bus search to provide detailed and easily navigable information.
  • Modern and interactive visual design for the whole app.
